02Sort current deals into three forecasting buckets

Use the strict definitions. Deals that don't cleanly land in any of the three are the ones you don't yet understand — surface them.

Committed
Verbal or written YES. Waiting only on signature or first payment. Not "they sounded enthusiastic" — actual commitment.
Best-case
Active deal. Engaged buyer. Realistic timing. NOT just "looking promising" — there's specific evidence of engagement.
Pipeline
Qualified but not yet active. They've passed the prospect bar but aren't currently in a live conversation cycle.
Don't cleanly fit
Deals you've been privately weighting at 50% or 70% but can't honestly place in any of the three buckets above. These are the ones you don't actually understand yet.
