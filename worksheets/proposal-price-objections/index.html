Tighten Your Proposal Move

Build the four-part shape. Practice the silence. Design the response pattern for the objection you hear most.

01Build the four-part shape

For a specific prospect you're currently working with. If you currently propose options, force yourself to pick the ONE.

1

Played back in their words, not yours. What you actually heard them describe in discovery.

2

ONE. Not a menu. The specific thing you propose, based on what you heard.

3

Stated once. Clearly. No hedge, no "but," no softener.

4

What happens if they say yes — today, not eventually.

02Practice presenting price

State the price, then count to seven silently before you say anything else. Five times before your next proposal call. Check each rep as you do it.

It will feel artificial the first time. That's the muscle being built.

03Design your response pattern

Pick the objection you hear most often. Write your response — acknowledge, ask a clarifying question, name what you're listening for underneath.

Which objection do you hear most often?

Short. Validates their statement without conceding anything. NOT "you're right" — usually closer to "I hear you" or "that makes sense."

Turns the objection into information. Asks WHAT or WHO or WHEN — not "why."

What does their answer to your question tell you? What pattern would tell you the objection is real vs. a softer "I'm not sure yet"?
