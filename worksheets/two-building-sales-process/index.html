Draw Your Skeleton. Run the Test.

Map the four stages of your sales process. Name the artifact each one produces. Then check whether a new hire could actually run it in week two.

01Draw the four-stage skeleton

For one specific motion you actually run. Name the entry and exit criteria sharply — vague criteria mean the stage isn't real yet.

Which motion is this for?

1
Prospect

What makes someone a prospect (vs. just a name)?

What tells you they're ready to move into discovery?

A concrete thing the rep can show you. A name + qualifying notes, a logged contact, etc.

2
Discover

What has to be true to start discovery (not just "we scheduled a call")?

What tells you discovery is done? (Hint: the criteria from lesson 2.)

Discovery notes, a captured shape, a recorded conversation, etc.

3
Propose

What has to be true to start the proposal? (Earned the right.)

What tells you the proposal is presented and a decision is being made?

A four-part proposal document, a presented offer, etc.

4
Close

What has to be true to attempt the close?

What tells you the deal is actually closed — not "they'll get back to us"?

Signed agreement + booked first action. Both, not one.
