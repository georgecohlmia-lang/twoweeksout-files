02

Business Operating Burn

Separate from your personal draw

What the business itself costs to operate each month — separate from what you pay yourself. Most early-stage owners land between $500 and $4,000. Significantly higher than that deserves a hard look — high burn in year one is one of the most predictable ways to run out of time before you run out of ideas.

Your Personal Runway

Available capital ÷ total monthly burn

Take every reliable dollar available to you — personal savings, startup capital, a spouse's dependable income — add it up, divide by total monthly burn. That's your runway in months. Write it on a sticky note and put it somewhere visible.

05

Owner Salary · Three Commitments

Your salary is a lever · use it deliberately

Every $1,000/month you reduce your draw — if your savings can absorb it — extends runway. That's not a reason to take nothing; taking nothing is unsustainable and leads to resentment. It's a reason to be deliberate. Answer the three questions explicitly. Write it down. Don't defer it indefinitely.
