Your Management Discipline

For one direct report — install the cadence, deliver one specific piece of feedback, do one delegation handoff cleanly, and commit to one growth move.

01The direct report

This worksheet builds the management discipline around one person. Name them so the artifact is real.

02The operating cadence — install it on the calendar

As an owner, you are the system. The cadence collapses the moment you stop personally enforcing it. Put it on the calendar before you close this worksheet.

Weekly 1:1

30 minutes, same time each week, owned by the report's agenda. Non-negotiable.

Weekly team meeting

One hour, focused on what is changing this week. Decisions, not status updates.

Monthly review

90 minutes per person. Looking back at the prior month and forward at the next.

03One piece of feedback to deliver this week

Direct, specific, in the moment. Name the behavior and the consequence. Don't save it up for a sit-down.

04One delegation handoff — done cleanly

Outcome. Constraints. Decision rights. Then follow through — the first time you override a decision they made within the boundaries you set, the delegation is dead.

05One coaching plan — develop the person, not just manage them

The three questions from the lesson, applied to this specific person. Then engineer the next quarter to deliver on what would actually move them.

06Performance-management timeline (only if applicable)

Only fill if a specific gap needs naming. The lesson's discipline: weeks, not months. The delay damages the team more than the underperformance itself.
