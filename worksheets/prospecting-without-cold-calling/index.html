Run the Diagnostic. Then the Engine.

Three questions to know whether you should add prospecting. The right referral ask, written down. The optional 10-name list, only if you've earned it.

01Run the conversion-first diagnostic

Three questions, in order. The answers tell you whether you should be adding prospecting at all.

Q1 · What's your close rate on qualified conversations?
Real number. From the dashboard you built in session 5. If you don't know it, write "don't know" — that's diagnostic information.
Q2 · How many qualified conversations per week?
A qualified conversation is one where the prospect has confirmed a real reason and could plausibly close. Not "calls had." Real conversations.
Q3 · Is referral or inbound producing some flow already?
Yes/no/some. If yes, you optimize that before adding a new channel.
02Write your three-part referral ask

Specific. Named. Cognitive load on you, not on them. Use it at the end of every close-worthy conversation this week.

1

Concrete attributes — role, situation, size, stage. Not "people like you" or "anyone interested."

2

In their language, not yours. Why would they take the meeting?

3

Not "do you know anyone." A specific named ask: "Is there one person who comes to mind?"

03If — and only if — earned: 10 named prospects

Build a list ONLY if your conversion is solid and your referral motion is flowing. Otherwise, skip this step.

Each prospect needs a specific real reason — something you know about their situation that connects to your offer. Templates defeat the purpose.
